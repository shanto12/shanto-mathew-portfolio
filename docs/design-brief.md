# Design Brief

## Purpose

Create a personal website for Shanto Mathew at `ShantoMathew.com` that presents public-safe achievements, skills, and a gallery of live Netlify-hosted demos.

## Audience

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating AI/security automation fit.
- Security leaders looking for SOAR/XSOAR/XSIAM and AI automation proof.
- Technical founders or platform teams evaluating forward-deployed AI engineering judgment.

## First Screen

The first viewport should make Shanto's identity obvious, position him as a senior AI security automation engineer and agentic AI builder, and route visitors to either the demo gallery or contact section.

## Visual Direction

Premium engineering portfolio. Light, crisp, high-trust, with teal/emerald security accents and restrained amber highlights. Avoid a generic resume page, dark-blue dominance, purple gradients, beige, or decorative filler.

## Data Safety

Do not publish phone number, birthdate, full address, identity documents, exact compensation or recruiter details, private opportunity history, internal client specifics, or secrets.
