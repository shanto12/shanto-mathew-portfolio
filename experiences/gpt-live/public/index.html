SHANTO MATHEWDFW, TX ↗
CURIOUS BY NATURE.
ENGINEER BY PRACTICE.

I work at the intersection of AI, security and the everyday work that connects them.

I started in testing and support, moved into Python engineering at Commonwealth Bank, and went deeper into security automation at NBN and Splunk. A chapter in data science at BHP added another way to ask questions. Today, I bring those experiences into applied AI and enterprise security work.

I’m most interested in the part between a promising idea and something another person can actually use. That means writing the Python, connecting the tools, thinking through failure, and paying attention to the handoff.
